Ad Speeder

Get through video ads faster

A free, open-source Chrome extension for YouTube and Prime Video.

  • Plays ads at 16× speed and mutes them.
  • Clicks Skip the moment it appears, including after the countdown and for back-to-back ads.
  • Restores your normal speed and sound as soon as the ad ends.
  • Collects no data. No tracking, no analytics, no network requests.

Install

Install from the Chrome Web Store (link coming soon), or load the extension/ folder as an unpacked extension.

Help

Found an ad it missed, or something broke? Open an issue.
